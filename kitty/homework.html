Start with this

Three things, in this order. Everything further down the page is there when these are comfortable, not before.

1

Every morning, five minutes

The voice set in your report. Before any other English, before coffee if you can.

2

Fifteen minutes daily: the same safe words on rotation

Ban your three most used adjectives for one week. The replacements arrive quickly.

3

Ten minutes, later in the day: concrete detail

Add one number, one name or one place to every answer. Keep it separate from the first one. Spacing beats stacking.
